Map Vote Dashboard

1

Get a Webhook URL

In Discord, go to your channel → Edit Channel → Integrations → Webhooks → New Webhook → Copy URL. Paste it in the Webhook field.

2

Add your maps

Enter each map name and an image URL. Right-click any image online and choose Copy image address to get a direct URL. Up to 10 maps per vote.

3

Customise & preview

Set your poll question, duration, and embed colour. The right panel shows a live preview of exactly what will appear in Discord.

4

Post to Discord

Click Post Maps + Poll. The intro, poll, and map images are sent to your channel. Discord enforces one vote per person automatically.

🔒 Your webhook URL never leaves your browser. All requests go directly from your browser to Discord — nothing is stored on any server. Everything is saved locally in your browser so your settings will be here next time you visit.
